WHY CALYPSO?

Small stack.
Clear purpose.

Calypso keeps the desktop focused: a Wayland compositor, a custom shell, dynamic theming, a fast launcher, and practical defaults — all carried by one visual language.

◈

Modern desktop

Hyprland and Aurora Dotfiles combine into a responsive, keyboard-friendly workspace.

✦

Crystal identity

The Calypso mark stays consistent across the website, ISO, installer, and desktop assets.

⌁

Dynamic themes

Matugen, wallpaper, Kitty, launcher, and shell styling are designed as one system.

↗

Open & remixable

Configs, build files, artwork, and desktop components stay open for experimentation.

AURORA DOTFILES

A desktop with a point of view.

The Calypso installer targets GNOME, KDE Plasma, Niri, and Hyprland. Aurora is currently being built for Hyprland first.

THE VISUAL SYSTEM

Emerald glass.
Black space.

Glowing edges, translucent surfaces, crystalline geometry, and restrained motion carry the same identity through the desktop and the web.

THE STACK

Wayland-first.

Hyprland, Quickshell, Kitty, Zsh, Fastfetch, Fuzzel, Matugen, Yazi, PipeWire, and practical system tooling.
