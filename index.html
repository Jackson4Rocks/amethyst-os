WHY CALYPSO?

Small stack.
Clear purpose.

Calypso keeps the desktop focused: a Wayland compositor, a custom shell, dynamic theming, a fast launcher, and practical defaults — wrapped in one visual language.

◈

Modern desktop

Hyprland and Aurora Dotfiles combine into a responsive, keyboard-friendly workspace.

✦

Crystal identity

The emerald faceted C is the canonical Calypso mark across the project and website.

⌁

Dynamic themes

Matugen, wallpaper, Kitty, launcher, and shell styling are designed as one system.

↗

Open & remixable

Configs, build files, artwork, and desktop components stay open for experimentation.
